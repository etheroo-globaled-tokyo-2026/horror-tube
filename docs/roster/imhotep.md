# Imhotep

- **name:** Imhotep
- **proposed ENS label:** `imhotep`
- **fandom URL:** https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Imhotep_(Universal_Monsters)
- **brief:** Egyptian high priest cursed and mummified alive; undead dark priest who stalks the reincarnation of his lost love.
- **role:** villain
- **strength:** 5 — Page lists mummy physiology (immortality, self-sustenance) and occult arts more than raw physical power.
- **intelligence:** 9 — Page lists brilliant intellect, deception, charisma, deep knowledge of ancient Egyptian history, and multilingualism.
- **luck:** 4 — Page states his goal to revive Ankh-es-en-amon failed, and he is defined by being cursed and mummified alive.
- **lore excerpt:** Main antagonist of the 1932 _The Mummy_: a high priest condemned to living mummification who returns with Egyptian occult arts (hypnosis, heart-stopping spells, necromancy via the Scroll of Thoth) to reclaim his love. Source: https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Imhotep_(Universal_Monsters)
