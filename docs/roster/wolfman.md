# The Wolf Man

- **name:** Lawrence Stewart Talbot (The Wolf Man)
- **proposed ENS label:** `wolfman`
- **fandom URL:** https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/The_Wolf_Man_(Universal_Monsters)
- **brief:** Scientist Larry Talbot, bitten by a werewolf named Bela; seeks to stop other monsters and lift his curse.
- **role:** hero
- **strength:** 8 — Page lists werewolf physiology including inhuman strength, durability, speed, stamina, and endurance.
- **intelligence:** 6 — Page gives his occupation as scientist (astronomy) before the curse.
- **luck:** 5 — Page lists an accelerated healing factor, but the biography centers on contracting an unwanted lycanthropy curse.
- **lore excerpt:** Titular protagonist of the 1941 film, he returns to Wales, is bitten by Bela, and as Lon Chaney's Larry Talbot hunts a way to end his curse while opposing other monsters. Source: https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/The_Wolf_Man_(Universal_Monsters)
