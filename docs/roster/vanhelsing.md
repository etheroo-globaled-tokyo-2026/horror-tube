# Abraham Van Helsing

- **name:** Professor Abraham Van Helsing
- **proposed ENS label:** `vanhelsing`
- **fandom URL:** https://universalmonsters.fandom.com/wiki/Abraham_Van_Helsing
- **brief:** Dutch doctor, lawyer, professor, and vampire hunter; arch-enemy of Count Dracula and protagonist of Stoker's _Dracula_.
- **role:** hero
- **strength:** 5 — Page quotes Seward describing him as medium weight but strongly built, with poise indicative of thought and power.
- **intelligence:** 9 — Page lists M.D., D.Ph., D.Litt., calls him one of the most advanced scientists of his day, philosopher and metaphysician with an open mind.
- **luck:** 5 — Page identifies him as human (not immortal) yet credits iron nerve and indomitable resolution as his equipment against Dracula.
- **lore excerpt:** Bram Stoker's Dutch polymath protagonist—doctor and vampire hunter—is Dracula's arch-enemy; Seward praises his nerve, resolution, and kindness in service of mankind. Source: https://universalmonsters.fandom.com/wiki/Abraham_Van_Helsing
