# Frankenstein's Monster

- **name:** Frankenstein's Monster
- **proposed ENS label:** `frankenstein`
- **fandom URL:** https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Frankenstein%27s_Monster_(Universal_Monsters)
- **brief:** Artificial being stitched from corpses and brought to life by lightning; wants love and acceptance, turns hostile after mistreatment.
- **role:** villain
- **strength:** 9 — Page lists superhuman strength, durability, endurance, indestructibility, and immortality.
- **intelligence:** 3 — Page states that for the later series the Monster was depicted as a shambling and mute idiot (Glenn Strange).
- **luck:** 6 — Page lists indestructibility and immortality among powers and skills.
- **lore excerpt:** Created by Henry Frankenstein from corpses and lightning, he only wants to be loved and fit into society, but mistreatment drives him to hostility and violence across the Universal films. Source: https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Frankenstein%27s_Monster_(Universal_Monsters)
